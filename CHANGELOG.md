# Changelog

## 0.1.0.2 — 2026-11-17

- Show selected chart-period price change in percent and dollars per share, comparing its first split-adjusted daily close with the newest available quote.
- Show the actual starting date, starting price and quote timestamp; unavailable comparisons do not invent a return.
- Switch light/dark directly from the header; retain persistent Auto, Light and Dark choices in Settings.

## 0.1.0.1 — 2026-11-17

- Automatically show one newest available quote per stock, with timing and market coverage behind a small information icon.
- Use readable month-day-year dates throughout financials, calendars, IPOs and charts; preserve calendar-day dates across time zones.
- Refresh the local walkthrough and add an original upbeat instrumental soundtrack.

## 0.1.0.0 — 2026-11-17

Initial private research application.

- Authenticated Admin/Viewer workspaces, account-isolated settings, protected sessions and request budgets.
- Five-year daily charts, configurable indicators, persisted presets and source-anchored research badges.
- Sector heatmaps, financial cards, news banners, earnings calendar and conditional upcoming IPOs.
- Separate latest-IEX and delayed-consolidated price caches, shared batch refreshes and automatic market-hours appearance.
- Source/cache failure handling, SEC issuer isolation, corporate-action observations, backup/restore and immutable local previews.
- Unit/API, browser regression and private CI checks. Provider secrets, cached market data and account credentials are excluded from source control.

Analyst coverage, FRED storage permission, wider market-data display rights and approved NAS/private-network deployment remain separate access requirements.
